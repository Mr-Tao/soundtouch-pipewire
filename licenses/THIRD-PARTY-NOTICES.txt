Rust GTK controller dependencies
--------------------------------

The optional GTK controller statically links third-party Rust crates recorded
in control/Cargo.lock. The complete x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu runtime graph,
selected license texts, crate versions, links, and copyright notices are in
RUST-DEPENDENCIES.html. It is generated reproducibly with cargo-about from
control/about.toml and control/about.hbs. Build-only and development-only
crates are not linked into the shipped controller and are excluded.

the supplemental terms governing use of the SoundTouch Connection.
